license material and active content. The real committed catalog is scanned;
this is not online publication or deployment.

separate frozen research tree. Its assertion that publication bytes equal the
old baseline is valid for that isolated repair, not for a combined deployment.

claim about that historical suite. New portable tests exercise the actual two
scripts without external research-tree dependencies. Existing commercial,
publication-preparation and catalog-renderer tests remain unchanged.

with external artifacts. Rebuild the installed wheel separately. Independent
