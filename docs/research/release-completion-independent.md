## Limits

The offline tool has no external trust anchor for execution, legal authority,
remote responses or representativeness. Different producer/auditor byte text
is not authenticated independence. Caller hashes freeze chosen bytes, not
truth. Model/check documents are inspected structurally, not independently
solved or numerically validated by this tool. No benchmark/commercial/public
coverage, genuine one-to-three-card scaling or deployment claim is made.
This review is not a hostile process sandbox or a formal all-input proof.
Actual clean test counts, findings and evidence pins are reported after runs.
